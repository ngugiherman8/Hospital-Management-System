# Quick guide: Cashier

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Open **Invoices**. Find the visit's invoice. Add or remove lines while it is a draft, then **Finalise** and print.
2. Take payment: cash, M-Pesa (type the transaction code) or card. Part payments are fine; the balance updates.
3. Give every payer a receipt. Receipt numbers are automatic; do not write your own on a printed receipt.
4. Refunds and voids need an administrator.
5. At the end of the day open **Cash-up**, count the cash, enter it, and explain any difference before you sign.

## If something goes wrong

- M-Pesa code typed wrong: tell the administrator. Do not take a second payment to fix it.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
