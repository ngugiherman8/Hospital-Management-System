# Quick guide: Manager

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. **Today's report** shows the day's visits and money. It can be read from the cloud copy as well.
2. **Weekly review** is the page for the weekly meeting: week figures, open problems, low stock, unexplained pilot-day differences.
3. **Problems** is where anyone reports a fault. Update status and the workaround here.
4. **Access review** lists refused requests, many wrong passwords, night-time access and heavy use. Ask the person first; unusual is not the same as wrong.

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot change clinical or billing records. That is by design.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
