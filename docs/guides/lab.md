# Quick guide: Laboratory

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Open **Lab work list**. It shows tests waiting, oldest first.
2. Collect the sample, mark it collected, and enter the result with its unit. The system flags high, low and critical results against the reference range.
3. A critical result needs a phone call to the doctor. Record who you spoke to and when.
4. Do not edit a released result. Enter a corrected result with a reason; the old one stays on record.

## If something goes wrong

- The reference range looks wrong for a test: tell the administrator. Do not work around it.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
