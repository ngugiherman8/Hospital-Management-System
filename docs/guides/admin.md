# Quick guide: Administrator

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Everything the manager sees, plus **Go or no-go**, **Training**, **Set-up check**, **Sync status**, and the Administration site.
2. Create accounts and roles in Administration, or load them from a file (`load_setup`). A new person must choose a password at first sign-in.
3. Approve voids, refunds, price changes and stock write-offs. Always type a reason.
4. Remove leavers' accounts every month and look at who has which role.
5. Check **Sync status** and `/ops/health/` each morning. Know where the backup key copy is kept.

## If something goes wrong

- Locked out: wait 15 minutes, or ask another administrator. Lost phone for two-factor: another administrator removes your device.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
