# Quick guide: Doctor

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Open the patient from **Today's queue**. Read allergies and the triage record first.
2. Write the consultation note and diagnosis. Order lab or imaging tests from the same page; each test becomes a charge by itself.
3. Prescribe from the drug list. The screen shows stock on hand. Check the dose before you save.
4. When the visit is complete, mark it completed so the bill can be finalised. Admit to a ward from the patient page when needed.
5. A critical result is shown as a red banner at the top of every page until someone acknowledges it.

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot see invoices or payments. That is by design.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
