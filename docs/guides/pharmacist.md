# Quick guide: Pharmacist

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Open **Dispensing**. Prescriptions waiting are listed; open one.
2. The system takes stock from the batch that expires first. If stock is short it dispenses part and the rest stays waiting. It never takes more than is on hand.
3. Receive stock in **Receive stock** against a purchase order or a delivery note. Enter batch number, expiry and quantity exactly as printed.
4. Use **Stock-take** to count the shelf; the system shows the difference. Adjustments and write-offs need a reason.
5. **Stock levels** shows drugs at or under reorder level and batches expiring within 90 days.

## If something goes wrong

- Wrong quantity dispensed: do not delete. Use an adjustment with the reason, so the record stays honest.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
