# Quick guide: Nurse

One page. Keep it at the workstation. Your super-user answers questions first; then IT.

## Your day

1. Open **Today's queue**, pick the next patient, and record **triage**: temperature, pulse, blood pressure, breathing rate, oxygen, weight, and priority. Emergency priority moves the patient up the queue.
2. Check allergies on the patient page before any medicine is given.
3. On the ward, use **Wards** to see beds, admit, move or discharge. Record observations and notes at the bedside.
4. Look at **Stock levels** to see if a drug is available before the doctor prescribes it.

## If something goes wrong

- Vital signs look dangerous: tell the doctor first, then record. The priority setting does not call anyone for you.
- Any problem at all: **Problems, Report a problem**. Do not type patient names; use the hospital number.
- Server down: use the paper forms (**Paper forms**), call IT, and type the paper in afterwards with the real time of each event.
- Always sign out when you leave the screen. Your account is your signature in the audit trail.
